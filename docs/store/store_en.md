## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
BU Kampus is an INDEPENDENT and UNOFFICIAL campus app built for students and staff of Bingol University. It is a personal project and has no corporate affiliation, partnership or endorsement from Bingol University. The university's name and trademarks belong to the institution.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• News, announcements and events: follow the content from the university's public news and announcement pages in one fast, readable place. Save news to your favorites.
• Dining menu: today's and this month's menu in your pocket.
• Discover: share a post with one photo and a short note, like posts from friends and your department, follow people.
• Chat: one-to-one and group chats with real-time messaging, photo sharing, replies, reactions and mute. Messages are stored encrypted on the server.
• Clubs and events: discover student clubs, join them, enter club and event chats; RSVP to events and check in with a QR code.
• Polls: take part in campus polls with a secret ballot.
• Search: search news, people, clubs and events.
• Notifications: you decide which notifications you get for messages, event reminders and announcements.
• Support and safety: report inappropriate content and users, block users, and send support requests.
• Three languages, two themes: Turkish, English and Arabic (with right-to-left layout); light and dark theme.

WHO CAN USE IT?

Sign-up requires a university e-mail address (bingol.edu.tr) and e-mail verification. The service is aimed at university students and staff; users under 18 may use it only with the knowledge and consent of a legal guardian (Terms of Use, article 4).

PRIVACY AND YOUR DATA

• You can delete your account from inside the app at any time; deletion anonymizes the account.
• You can download your data (data export).
• Location is not collected. Contacts are used only if you allow it, and only e-mail hashes are used to find friends; your raw address book is never sent to the server.
• There are no ads, and your data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.
• Privacy policy and account deletion: https://bukampus.com.tr/en/privacy and https://bukampus.com.tr/en/delete-account

IMPORTANT

News, announcements and menu content come from the university's public websites; please treat the source website as authoritative for accuracy and timeliness. This app is not a public service or an official information channel.

Questions: use the Support section in the app or <SUPPORT_EMAIL>
```
