# Stitch istemi (BU Widget - events)

Stitch projesi: "BU Widget - events" (stitch.withgoogle.com, kullanicinin hesabi). Tek istem, App modu:

> Design a presentation board for a home-screen widget family named "Yaklasan Etkinlikler" (Upcoming Events) of a Turkish university student app "BU Kampus" (Bingol Universitesi). Premium Monochrome style: pure black and white only, red only for danger, NO brand color, single typeface Inter, NO bold text (max weight 400, hierarchy by size/tone/spacing), thin 1px borders, rounded corners 22pt, calm premium, not a crowded AI card. Dark theme: background #09090B, surface #18181B, text white, secondary #A1A1AA. Show three iOS widgets stacked on a dark neutral canvas: Small 158x158pt (...), Medium 338x158pt (up to 3 rows ...), Large 338x354pt (3 rows with roomy layout plus a header with small BU emblem ...). Realistic Turkish content: "Yapay Zeka Soylesisi - Bilgisayar Toplulugu - 14:00 Konferans Salonu", "Bahar Senligi Hazirlik - Muzik Kulubu - 17:30 Ogrenci Merkezi", "Fotograf Yuruyusu - Fotograf Kulubu - 10:00 Kampus Girisi". Long titles truncate with ellipsis. No other users' names or photos.

Cikti: `screen.png` (pano goruntusu), `code.html` (Stitch'in uretdigi HTML). Stitch tek karanlik pano uretti; Android + acik tema + 3x PNG ciktilari ayni tasarim dilinden `render.html` (Inter, Lucide 1.5px ikonlar, gercek pt/dp olculeri; `?w=events&p=ios|android&s=small|medium|large&t=light|dark&e=0|1`) ile uretildi (Playwright, zoom 3 = 3x piksel). Ana ekran maketleri `make_home_mockups.py` (Pillow). Stitch'in kendi yazi boyutlari (10-11 px) gercek widget icin cok kucuktu; render.html'de buyutuldu (README'ye bakin). Stitch DESIGN.md kullanilmadi; belirtimin tek kaynagi `../README.md`.
